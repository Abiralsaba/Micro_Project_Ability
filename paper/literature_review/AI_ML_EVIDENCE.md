# Evidence notes for the AI/ML assessment

**Date/cutoff:** 15 September 2026. This is a targeted follow-up to the AMP literature review. It evaluates research options rather than claiming an exhaustive search or a verified absence of equivalent methods. [Recorded queries](AI_ML_SEARCH_LOG.json) use general web discovery; Scopus and Web of Science were not directly searched. Exact result counts are unavailable.

Read the recommendation in [AI_ML_NOVELTY_ANALYSIS.md](AI_ML_NOVELTY_ANALYSIS.md). Statements about proposed AMP models, experiments and likely usefulness are the assessment's judgments; they are not results attributed to these papers.

## Uncertainty and claims audit

- No model, dataset, measured improvement, universal participant count or journal acceptance probability has been invented.
- Named algorithms are candidates or baselines; existing method names are not presented as new AMP algorithms.
- No new DOI is guessed. Official proceedings/publisher/author URLs identify these sources.
- Abstract-only sources support broad functionality only. Full-text labels refer to the specified examined sections, not independent replication.
- Fugu/Decima concern other domains; their role is methodological prior art. Adapting a comparator must be disclosed.
- SIGMA-ASL is a preprint; it is not silently treated as a peer-reviewed dataset paper or a ready-to-use dataset for Ability.
- Standard conformal coverage is not transferred unqualified to correlated, action-dependent sessions.
- Automated hardware traces, user-progression labels and comprehension outcomes remain distinct.
- The user's latest data status is authoritative: devices work; a structured research dataset has not yet been collected.
- Novelty remains conditional on a wider search and on a mechanism that beats competent, equally informed conventional/composed baselines.
